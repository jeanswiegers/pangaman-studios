Privacy policy

Last updated 19 October 2026.

Short version: our tools collect nothing and send nothing, and this website doesn't track you.

Our Unity tools

CoreCLR Ready (Lite and Pro) runs entirely inside your Unity editor. It has no telemetry or analytics and makes no network connections. It doesn't use accounts or licence checks of its own.

  • It reads your project's C# files (and, in Pro, managed DLLs) to analyse them on your computer.
  • It writes files only when you ask it to: a report you export, and in Pro the files you choose to fix plus their backups in your project's Library/CoreClrReady/Backups folder. You can delete those at any time.
  • The Unity docs button opens a Unity documentation page in your web browser when you click it. That page is Unity's, under Unity's privacy policy.

This website

This site is static HTML. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no scripts, no web fonts and no content from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Purchases

Our tools are sold through the Unity Asset Store. Unity handles payment and your account; we receive the information Unity shares with publishers under its terms, and use it only for licensing and support.

When you contact us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and to fix the problem you report. We don't add you to mailing lists and don't share your messages. Ask us at any time to delete them: SUPPORT_EMAIL.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
