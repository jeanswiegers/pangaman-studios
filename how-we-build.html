How we build

Our tools are built with AI assistance and reviewed by a person.

We use AI coding assistants to help write code, tests and documentation. A person at Pangaman Studios decides what each tool should do, reviews the changes, and is responsible for what we ship.

What that means in practice:

  • Every rule ships with automated tests against real Unity versions, and those tests must pass before a release.
  • Automatic fixes are conservative: when a change could alter behaviour, the tool reports the problem instead of fixing it. In Pro, fixes are shown as a diff before anything changes, backed up, and can be reverted.
  • Screenshots on this site are real captures of the tool. Marketing images are designed from those captures.
  • If something is wrong, it's our mistake to fix. Please tell us.
