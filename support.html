Support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests are welcome. A person reads every message.

Contact

Email jeanswiegers@gmail.com. We usually reply within two business days.

You can also reach us through our publisher page on the Unity Asset Store.

What to include

  • The product and edition (Lite or Pro) and its version (Package Manager shows it).
  • Your Unity version and editor platform (Windows, macOS or Linux).
  • For a wrong or missing finding: the exported Markdown report (Export MD), and if you can, a small code sample that shows it. Please remove anything you don't want to share.
  • For an error: the lines starting with [CoreCLR Ready] from the Console or Editor.log.

Before you write

  • The manual covers installation, every rule, the command line, applying and reverting fixes, and known limitations.
  • If a static value is meant to persist between Play sessions, that's not a bug: put // CoreClrReady:ignore on the line above it, or mark it [NoAutoStaticsCleanup].
  • Pro keeps backups in Library/CoreClrReady/Backups. Revert last restores the last batch of fixes.
